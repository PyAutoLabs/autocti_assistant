## 2026-07-17 — base wiki compiled (Phase 3)

Initial CTI literature wiki, built from public sources with citations verified
against arXiv / ADS / journal pages. Bibliography seeded with eight papers
covering: pixel-based and forward-model CTI correction on HST ACS
(Massey 2010, Anderson & Bedin 2010, Massey 2014, Israel 2015), the analytical
Euclid/Gaia CTI model (Short 2013), trap pumping for Euclid VIS (Skottfelt 2017),
and CTI as a weak-lensing shape systematic (Massey 2013).

Source pages: `cti_detector_physics`, `cti_correction_algorithms`,
`euclid_vis_calibration`, `hst_acs_cti`, `trap_pumping`, `cti_shape_bias`.
